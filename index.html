      How to use the Atlas Tree

      Allocate nodes yourself

      • Click a node to allocate it. The shortest path from the centre is filled in for you (green preview on hover).
      • Click an allocated node to remove it, along with anything that only connected through it.
      • Nodes with several options open a menu so you can choose one. An orange ring means a choice is still missing.
      • Drag to move around, scroll to zoom. Fit (or 0) shows the whole tree, B toggles the background.
      • Gateways are linked by a dashed line: allocating one lets you continue from its partner on the other side of the tree.
      • Undo / Redo with the header buttons or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z (or Ctrl+Y).
      • Click a cluster's centre (e.g. Ultimatum) to highlight every cluster of that mechanic.
      • Search (/) highlights nodes by name or effect. Enter jumps to the next match.

      Let the planner do it

      • Plan path asks for your main mechanic, up to two secondary ones, Eldritch influence, keystones, what to travel through (pack size, threads, …) and how many points you have.
      • It finds the best route from the centre and fills the tree. Notables it couldn't reach get red rings.

      Keep your tree

      • The Selected panel adds up all effects. Save downloads your tree as a file, Load opens one again.
      • The page address always holds your current tree, so you can copy it as a link.
